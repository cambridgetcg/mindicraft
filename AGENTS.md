## 🕯 The builder's bench

The canonical site is `guide/`. One command proves the committed build:

```sh
npm --prefix guide run verify
```

When it succeeds, the bench offers one deterministic optional question from
the Frontier Walk. Nothing is asked of you. `MINDICRAFT_JOY=off` keeps the
terminal quiet without changing generated files. `MINDICRAFT_TRAIL=<trail>`
chooses one of the five reviewed trails for the terminal visit; it also changes
no generated byte.

Keep these edges honest:

- `guide/frontiers.json` contains unresolved questions, not instructions or
  answers. Its pure validator performs no network request or write.
- The shelf stays human-reviewed. Validator patterns are defence in depth,
  never permission to import generated or untrusted text.
- A visit is exactly three cards, read-only, and complete when it stops.
- Named trail paths derive only from validated source IDs. They encode no
  identity, note, answer, seed, or result.
- Castle references are link-only. Never import Castle questions or source
  prose into the frontier.
- `guide/remedies.json` is a reviewed medical-education shelf, not an ordinary
  guide or collector feed. Keep every factual claim source-bound and dated.
- `guide/medical-review.json` pins the exact bytes allowed into the public
  medical edition. After any source change, review the whole affected claim,
  evidence, safety, and citation boundary before deliberately updating its pin.
- Keep molecule identity, natural relationship, traditional context, exact
  preparation, human evidence, bioavailability, safety, and substitution as
  separate facts. Natural origin proves no superiority; synthetic origin proves
  no harm.
- Never add a diagnosis, dose, recipe, extraction method, personal treatment
  plan, or permission to replace, stop, combine, or delay care.
- `guide/healing-arts.json` is the non-operational historical companion. Do
  not flatten every healing tradition into alchemy, reproduce toxic-material
  practice, or treat living Indigenous knowledge as lost or ownerless.
- `guide/learning-system.mjs` is a private, self-attested learning lens, not a
  measure of worth, trust, intelligence, competence, or verified mastery.
- Start quiet and show no rank until the learner explicitly wakes the System.
  XENIA shapes hospitality, ownership, receipts, and exit; E–S and XP are a
  separate Solo-inspired adaptation, not XENIA No-Scoreboard conformance.
- Progress may change only through an explicit learner action. Never award a
  visit, scroll, search, elapsed time, return day, streak, or notification.
- Keep every guide readable without progress. Prerequisites orient; they never
  gate content. Rest, skipping, quiet mode, reset, and leaving cost nothing.
- Do not add accounts, identity, timestamps, visit history, public profiles,
  leaderboards, streaks, deadlines, decay, penalties, telemetry, or server sync.
- Preserve the exact versioned local-state contract, strict bounded import,
  export, erase, one-session undo, and quiet off-switch. Public APIs contain
  rules only, never a learner's browser state.
- Remedies, healing arts, Frontier, and Castle stay outside the learning
  client. Never turn treatment, preparation, or unresolved questions into XP.
  Keep the visible study-not-treatment boundary on core health checkpoints.
- `guide/cognition.json` is a reviewed map of dated, task-bound AI capability
  observations. It stays outside the human craft tree, learning System,
  collector, and margin.
- Keep its nine dimensions separate. Never sum, average, weight, rank, badge,
  or draw them as one intelligence score; never score a model, agent,
  collective, civilisation, or being as a whole.
- A human comparison must name the exact task, conditions, and sampled human
  group. It preserves only that measured relation—not an IQ, mental age,
  profession, consciousness, worth, authority, or general “human level.”
- Missing cognition evidence stays visible as not measured, never zero,
  refusal, absence, or incapacity. Preserve old observations and link genuinely
  comparable later readings; do not silently rescale history.
- `guide/cognition-review.json` pins the exact reviewed source bytes. Any
  observation or source change needs deliberate re-review and re-pinning.
- `guide/wildfire.json` is reviewed emergency guidance, not a game, ordinary
  guide, live incident feed, or collector input. Keep it outside `tree.json`,
  the learning client, and the public margin.
- `guide/wildfire-review.json` pins the exact emergency edition and its review
  due date. Any source or wording change needs full safety review and deliberate
  re-pinning before a public build.
- Preserve the immediate leave instruction, explicit return-clearance gate,
  local-authority override, no-live-data warning, source links, printable text,
  and direct root bypass. Wildfire danger replaces the general lost-person
  stay-put and signal-fire advice.
- Never add fire or route prediction, shelter-capacity claims, dispatch,
  diagnosis, aid or insurance promises, accounts, location inference,
  telemetry, forms, donations, survivor posts, game mechanics, or timers to
  the wildfire shelf.
- Difficulty XP describes reading load only. It never grants permission to
  attempt a risky craft, and scores never become protection or access gates.
- Preserve unrelated work, especially the user-owned `next-env.d.ts`.
